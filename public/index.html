Harry's Beverage Planogram & Cooler Merchandising Rules

1. The "Coke with Coke" Principle:

Organize cold vaults by strict brand blocks. Place Coca-Cola Original Taste, Coca-Cola Zero Sugar, and Diet Coke together in consecutive vertical columns. Never interleave non-Coca-Cola brands.

2. Zero Sugar Adjacency:

Merchandise Zero Sugar varieties directly to the immediate right of their regular-sugar counterpart (e.g. Sprite Zero Sugar sits immediately to the right of Sprite Regular).

3. Vertical Size & Packaging Hierarchy:

• Eye Level (Shelves 2–4): 20 oz single-serve plastic bottles and 16 oz energy cans.
• Top Shelf: Specialty glass bottles (Mexican Coke / Sprite) and premium waters.
• Bottom Shelf / Vault Floor: 2-Liter bottles and 12-pack / 15-pack can multi-packs.

4. FIFO Stock Rotation (First-In, First-Out):

Always load stock from the back of the walk-in cold vault so oldest expiration dates are pulled first.

5. Facing & Pull-Forward Standards:

During maintenance rounds, pull front bottles forward to the shelf lip and ensure all brand logos face forward.
