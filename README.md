# Harry's Convenience Stores — Ready-to-Drink Beverage Training System

Official beverage training catalog, cold vault planogram guide, and interactive inventory spreadsheet for **Harry's Convenience Stores** (Family-Owned & Operated Since 1991 in Tennessee and Georgia).

---

## 🥤 Overview

This interactive system was engineered from the store's Coca-Cola (myCoke) inventory order records. It catalogs all **276 active Ready-to-Drink (RTD) bottled and canned beverage SKUs** with genuine product photos cropped tightly into tiny, crisp thumbnails and saved with clean, descriptive drink names.

## 🖨️ Ultra-Compact Printable Training Guide (Minimal Paper Usage)

- **Dedicated Training View & Print Mode:**
  - 3-column / 4-column ultra-dense micro-card layout.
  - Tiny drink thumbnail (22–26px) positioned directly to the left of the drink name.
  - Includes Drink Name, Size, Container, Packaging, and SKU number.
  - Fits up to **75 items per page**, allowing the **entire 276-item catalog to print in just ~4 sheets of paper** (or 2 double-sided sheets).
  - Trainee & Trainer Sign-off verification header.

## 📊 Excel Spreadsheet Capabilities

- **Interactive Sheet Tabs:** Master Catalog (All 276), Colas & Soft Drinks (85), Energy Drinks (83), Sports & Hydration (40), Waters & Wellness (27), Juices & Teas (26), Dairy & Coffee (15).
- **Inline Editing:** Double-click any cell to edit descriptions, pack sizes, SKUs, or cooler doors.
- **Export Options:** Native `.xlsx` Microsoft Excel multi-sheet workbook export via SheetJS, CSV export, and file importer.
- **Planogram Merchandising Rules Modal:** Guidelines on "Coke with Coke", Zero Sugar adjacency, vertical size hierarchy, and FIFO stock rotation.
